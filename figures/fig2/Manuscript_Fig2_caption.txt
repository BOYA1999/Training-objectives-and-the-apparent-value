**Fig. 2. Training objectives and fixed-budget CYP2C9 assay allocation.** Four outcome-conditioned boundary examples illustrate changes in the Octant CYP2C9 top-150 shortlist (750 measured candidates). Panels a–b show the absolute-to-squared FP change; panels c–d show squared-to-interval FP. Row summaries cover the complete shortlist exchanges. Each panel shows nine feature–objective configurations. Circles denote seed 20261120 (filled, selected; open, outside); crosses denote four other seeds. Shading marks the top 150. Lower rank numbers indicate higher priority. Measured pIC50 and released 95% credible intervals (CrIs) appear beside each structure: supported hits have lower bounds ≥6, and below-threshold cases have upper bounds <6. Seed variation describes fitted-model ranks, not predictive uncertainty or assay CrIs. Cases were selected near the new FP selection boundary, conditional on their measured outcome and entry/exit status. They are illustrative, not representative samples or one-for-one molecular replacements.
